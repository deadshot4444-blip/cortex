# Lab reference slides: attribution

Real photomicrographs of Bielschowsky-stained human brain tissue, cropped from figures of open-access articles published under Creative Commons Attribution licenses. Each file is an observed-reference image of a published slide; none is the simulation's output. Credits are shown under each picture in the lab.

## Sources

- **S-ikonomovic-2020**: Ikonomovic MD, Buckley CJ, Abrahamson EE, Kofler JK, Mathis CA, Klunk WE, Farrar G. Post-mortem analyses of PiB and flutemetamol in diffuse and cored amyloid-β plaques in Alzheimer's disease. Acta Neuropathol. 2020;140:649-661. doi:10.1007/s00401-020-02175-1. PMC7498488. Open access, CC BY 4.0 (© The Author(s) 2020). Fig. 4 panels d and h used: Bielschowsky silver stain of frontal cortex, AD case 27, sequential 8 µm paraffin sections also stained for amyloid-β (4G8).
- **S-liu-2022**: Liu F, Sun J, Wang X, Jin S, Sun F, Wang T, Yuan B, Qiu W, Ma C. Focal-type, but not diffuse-type, amyloid beta plaques are correlated with Alzheimer's neuropathology, cognitive dysfunction, and neuroinflammation in the human hippocampus. Neurosci Bull. 2022. doi:10.1007/s12264-022-00927-5. PMC9554074. Open access, CC BY 4.0 (© The Author(s) 2022). Fig. 4 'Neuritic plaque' column used: modified Bielschowsky staining of hippocampus, the authors' 'normal' group (A) and AD group (B, C); scale bars 50 µm.
- **S-marengo-2022**: Marengo L, Armbrust F, Schoenherr C, Storck SE, Schmitt U, Zampar S, Wirths O, et al. Meprin β knockout reduces brain Aβ levels and rescues learning and memory impairments in the APP/lon mouse model for Alzheimer's disease. Cell Mol Life Sci. 2022;79:168. doi:10.1007/s00018-022-04205-5. PMC8891209. Open access, CC BY 4.0 (© The Author(s) 2022). Fig. 1A 'Bielschowsky' column used: Bielschowsky staining kit on FFPE frontal isocortex from an AD patient and an age-matched non-demented control; scale bar 100 µm.
- **S-zhang-2025**: Zhang QL, Liu Y, Tu T, Yan XX. The comorbidity of AD and PD: exploring clinical, pathological, and biomarker interactions. Aging Dis. 2025. doi:10.14336/AD.2025.0301. PMC13256385. Open access, Creative Commons Attribution License. Fig. 1 panels B and C used: Bielschowsky silver stain of the middle temporal gyrus (diffuse amyloid plaques) and of the hippocampus (a cored compact plaque) in a 70-year-old man with a clinical diagnosis of Parkinson's disease.
